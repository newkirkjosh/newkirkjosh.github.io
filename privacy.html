Privacy Policy

This policy covers rclone, a personal Google Cloud app used only by its owner to back up their own files.

  • The app requests only the drive.file scope: it can see and change only files it created in the owner's Google Drive, or files the owner gave it.
  • It runs on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google Drive.
  • No personal data is collected, stored elsewhere, sold, or shared with anyone.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
